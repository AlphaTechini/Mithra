## Goal
Approvers land on an inbox of pending proposals and open the shared cycle page from it. Holders open an invite link, connect (LocalNet: the role switcher; MainNet: Grofty, M10), accept their units, turn on auto-receive, and see their home: units, share, total received, next payment date and their payment history with transaction links, and accept a pending payment. Transaction links open an in-app transaction detail on LocalNet. Nothing on a holder screen ever shows another holder's name, units or amounts, even in errors.
